"Dad, how much horsepower does this one have? Which car is faster in a race?" "Papa, wie viel PS hat der hier? Welches Auto würde bei einem Rennen gewinnen?"

What started during Christmas 2025 to answer my sons' questions turned into a focused collector companion.
