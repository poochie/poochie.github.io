How it works

Features I actually wanted as a collector.

Universal AI Scanner

Carded, loose, or even real life 1:1 cars. Just point your camera. The AI identifies the casting, series, and even estimates real-world tech specs instantly.

Global Hunt Radar

See a live, privacy-safe map of community-spotted Super Treasure Hunts (STH) and Chases around your area. Perfect for your next toy hunt.

Car Compare (Top Trumps)

Match up vehicles from your garage head-to-head based on BHP, top speed, and 0-100 acceleration to settle the debate.

Smart Inventory

Never buy a duplicate by mistake again. Track quantities, variants, and monitor your collection's stats directly from your personal dashboard.
