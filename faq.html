Capturing Cars & Identification

There are three ways: 1. Live OCR Scan: The camera reads the text on the card. 2. AI Image Scan: The AI analyzes a photo of the casting. 3. Manual Reference: Search the global database and add it with one click.

Identifying these cars is incredibly difficult. Many models have been re-released over decades with different codes. While the AI is smart, there is still room for improvement, especially with Premium models and special editions. Optimal results require good lighting and a highly readable blister card without heavy glare.

The Toy ID (e.g., JKY53) is the unique fingerprint of the car. It is the most critical attribute in the entire system to determine the exact release year, variation, and series.

Technical Specs & AI Art

They are a Best Guess. For real-world licensed cars, the AI fetches realistic data. For Fantasy toy cars, the AI estimates specs based on engine type and design. No claim to 100% accuracy; it's built for the fun factor!

The images generated under AI Art are fake. The AI tries to create a cool artistic interpretation based on the visual prompt, but it can generate bad or completely wrong images (e.g., wrong spoilers, weird decals).
