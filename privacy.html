Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Welcome to Garage Prime. I built this app to help collectors track their inventory. Your privacy is extremely important to me. This policy explains how your data is handled.

1. Data Collection & Camera Usage

To use the core functionality of Garage Prime, the app requires access to your device's camera. Images taken are processed securely by AI providers (such as Google Vision/Gemini API) solely for the purpose of identifying the vehicle. We do not use your images to identify individuals.

2. Location Data (Global Hunt Radar)

If you choose to enable location sharing for the "Global Hunt Radar", the app will record the location where a vehicle was scanned. For your privacy: We automatically anonymize this data before displaying it on the global map. Other users will only see the city (e.g., "Los Angeles"), never your exact street address or coordinates.

3. Accounts & Subscriptions

When you create an account, we store your basic profile info. If you purchase a subscription or fuel pack, the payment is processed securely by Google Play and managed via RevenueCat. Garage Prime does not process or store your credit card information.

4. Third-Party Services & Ads

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Supabase: For secure database hosting and authentication.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying rewarded video ads. AdMob may use advertising IDs to serve personalized ads (unless you opt out in your device settings).
  • RevenueCat: For tracking purchase entitlements.

5. Account Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data. You can do this directly within the app settings or by contacting me. Once requested, your garage data and profile will be permanently erased.
