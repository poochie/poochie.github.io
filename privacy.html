Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data Collection

We collect information to provide better services to our users. For example, when you scan a vehicle, the image may be processed by our AI providers to identify the casting.

2. Location Data (Global Hunt Radar)

If you choose to use the Global Hunt feature, we store coordinates of scanned items. For your privacy, we only display the city name publicly, never your exact street address.

3. Subscriptions & Data Deletion

If you purchase a subscription or fuel pack, payment is processed securely via Google Play/RevenueCat. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app settings.
